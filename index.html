About Us

Built on Trust, Texas Work Ethic & Straight Talk

SICG, LLC serves Southeast Texas with honest timelines, clear communication, and craftsmanship you can count on. Most days we're on residential jobs — additions, remodels, and repairs for families who'd rather improve than move.

When commercial work comes our way, it's usually a neighborhood restaurant, a strip-mall tenant build-out, or a small retail space — the kind of projects that keep local business growing. We're proud to work where we live.

What We Do

Services

🏗

General Construction

From room additions and whole-home remodels to restaurant dining rooms and strip-mall storefronts — we handle projects of every size. Residential work is our bread and butter; local commercial jobs keep us connected to the community.

📐

Design / Build

SICG, LLC is dedicated to reaching the deadlines set forth for every project. We provide a single source for both the design and construction services in order to meet clients' goals. By decreasing the number of outside entities involved, we can minimize the possibility of confusion and miscommunications.

🏠

Residential Services

Many times, your home is your biggest investment — a place for your family to live. In today's economic times, it's more economical (and much less stressful) to remodel or add on to your home instead of hoping that your home will sell.
